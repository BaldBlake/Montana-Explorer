About

What is it?

A free interactive map of Montana's public lands, wildlife, weather, and trails — built on public agency data. Runs entirely in your browser; no apps to install.

Where does the data come from?

BLM, USFS, Montana FWP, NOAA, and Open-Meteo. All sources are named on the About page.

How private is it?

No analytics, no third-party tracking, and no ads. Your saved pins live only on this device unless you sign in to sync them.

What's on the map

🟧

BLM land

Every Bureau of Land Management parcel in Montana, fed live from the BLM National SMA service. Click anywhere to find out if a point is BLM-administered.

🐻

Bear habitat

Official MT FWP grizzly recovery zones, 2022 estimated occupied range, and the generalized black/grizzly distribution layers. Click for likelihood at any spot.

🏕

Campsites & trails

USFS developed campsites plus the National Forest System Trail network and Motor Vehicle Use Map for off-road routes.

🌧

Live NEXRAD radar

Current precipitation radar refreshed every 5 minutes, with smooth zoom interpolation and zoom-friendly tiles.

🔮

4-hour storm forecast

Bilinear-interpolated rainfall forecast across Montana from Open-Meteo — see where weather is heading hours before it arrives.

💨

Wind & temperatures

Live wind direction and speed arrows across the state, plus current temperature badges over every major city.
