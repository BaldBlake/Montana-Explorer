About Montana Explorer

Montana Explorer is a free, open interactive map that brings together public data on Montana's public lands, wildlife, weather, and trails. It runs entirely in your browser — no tracking, no ads, no backend of our own. Sign in to sync your saved pins across every device.

Data sources

  • BLM land — BLM National Surface Management Agency (BLM Only) cached tile + feature service.
  • Bear habitat — Montana Fish, Wildlife & Parks Open Data Hub: Black & Grizzly Distribution, Grizzly Recovery Zones, Estimated Occupied Range 2022.
  • USFS campsites & trails — USFS Enterprise Data Warehouse: Recreation Opportunities, National Forest System Trails, Motor Vehicle Use Map Trails.
  • Live radar — NEXRAD base reflectivity tiles via Iowa State Mesonet.
  • Storm forecast — hourly precipitation grid from Open-Meteo, rendered with bilinear interpolation.
  • Weather & wind — current conditions, 7-day forecast, and grid wind from Open-Meteo.
  • Geocoding (search) — Nominatim (OpenStreetMap).
  • Base maps — OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, and Esri World Imagery (satellite).
  • Montana boundary — U.S. Census via usstatesgeojson.

How features work

Click for details

Right-click anywhere on the map to open a popup with the exact coordinates, current weather, a 7-day forecast, whether the point is on BLM land (and which administrative unit), and the bear-encounter likelihood.

Bear likelihood

The tier comes from the MT FWP services in this order:

  • Very High — inside an official Grizzly Recovery Zone (NCDE, Greater Yellowstone, Cabinet–Yaak, etc.)
  • High — inside the 2022 Grizzly Estimated Occupied Range
  • Moderate — inside a Generalized Distribution polygon (black bear, grizzly, or both)
  • Low — outside all mapped ranges

BLM identify

The yellow BLM tiles come from BLM's cached map service. When you click, we query the feature layer at that exact point and read back the ADMIN_UNIT_NAME field so the popup can show the parcel's name. At zoom 9 and above the map also drops "BLM" badges on each parcel's centroid (capped to keep things readable).

Storm forecast (next 4 hr)

Open-Meteo is queried for hourly precipitation forecasts on a 24×16 grid across Montana. For every screen pixel we bilinearly interpolate between the four nearest grid samples, then color the pixel by peak precipitation in the next four hours. The result is a smooth continuous wash, not blocky cells.

Saved pins & accounts

If you are signed in, pins are stored in your account on Supabase and sync to every browser you log in on. If you are not signed in, pins fall back to your browser's localStorage under the key mt-map-pins-v1 — that means they live on that device only.

Privacy

Montana Explorer has no analytics, no third-party scripts, and no advertising. The only data we collect is what you choose to save while signed in (your email, username, and the coordinates/labels of your pins). All of it is stored in your Supabase project and is only readable by you — admins can list users for account management but cannot see the contents of your private pins unless you make them so.
